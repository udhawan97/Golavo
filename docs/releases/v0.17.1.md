# Golavo v0.17.1 — Keep your place, keep the choice explicit

Golavo 0.17.1 is a focused continuity and accessibility release. It keeps the existing
forecast, provenance, privacy, and local-data boundaries unchanged while making the paths
between Matchday, the Match Cockpit, Model Lab, My Season, and Settings safer to navigate.

## Fixed

- **Text-safe mobile navigation.** All four primary destinations remain distinct at
  320 px with the XL reading setting. Multi-word labels wrap inside their own target;
  focus rings and the 375 px, tablet, and desktop layouts remain separate.
- **History-aware return.** Back from a Match Cockpit restores the Matchday card that
  opened it. Forward restores the cockpit, while a deliberate new destination starts
  at its heading.
- **Explicit local-data cancellation.** Removing follow history, all correction
  proposals, or one evidence capture now presents separate Cancel and destructive
  actions. Cancel or Escape disarms the choice and restores focus. No data is removed
  until the separately named destructive action is activated.
- **Keyboard focus continuity.** Escape closes Reading Comfort and returns focus to
  the `Aa` trigger.
- **Discoverable wide tables.** Track record, Backtests, My Season, and season outlook
  show a “More columns” cue and edge fade while horizontal content remains. The cue
  clears at the right edge, and overflow regions can receive keyboard focus.

## Trust boundaries

- This release changes presentation and navigation only. Forecast numbers, model
  authority, sealed artifacts, data-source rules, network consent, and license
  isolation are unchanged.
- Follow history, correction proposals, and evidence remain local. Their removal
  scopes are stated before confirmation; cancellation never mutates data.
- Installers remain OS-unsigned and not notarized, so Gatekeeper or SmartScreen may
  warn on first install. Release payloads and checksums retain Golavo's existing
  signed verification path.

## Verification

The release gate covers focused regression tests at 320/375 px, Back/Forward scroll
restoration, keyboard focus, all three destructive confirmation paths, and table-cue
state, followed by the full Python, UI, browser, documentation, Rust, provenance,
packaging, and release-artifact checks.
