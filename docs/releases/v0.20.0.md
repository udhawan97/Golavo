# Golavo v0.20.0 — Identity before inference

Golavo 0.20.0 turns the club and player context added after v0.19.0 into one bounded
desktop milestone. The statistical engine remains the only forecasting authority; the new
readings organize verified local or explicitly fetched provider evidence without inventing a
number, profile, ranking, or recommendation.

## What is new

- **Exact-identity team dossiers.** Open a club from My Teams or a current-season form
  board to read its certified observed record, every available season-model voice, and
  competition-scoped ratings, strength, workload, and run-in context in separate layers.
  A missing team or model-voice identity withholds the comparison instead of filling gaps.
- **Selected-match player dossiers.** A player in an already-fetched Sportmonks Player
  Lens response can open a fixture-only reading of provider-supplied match values. Opening
  the dossier performs no additional request or write, and missing values remain unavailable.

## Reliability and recovery

- Route and data handoffs are bound to the exact team, fixture, player, and provider
  identities that opened them, so a late response cannot populate a newer selection.
- Existing seals are verified before reuse. Release inventories are source-locked, and the
  updater preserves recovery journals across interrupted restore, checkpoint retirement,
  and missing-seal paths rather than accepting a partially verified state.
- Release actions and supported runtime, UI, and documentation dependencies were refreshed.
  The deterministic match index remains byte-identical after the release-environment rebuild.

## Trust boundaries

- Model voices stay separate. Dossiers never average them into a consensus or turn a season
  simulation into a sealed match forecast.
- Sportmonks remains optional BYOK, foreground-only, attributed, and no-store. Its values
  cannot enter Golavo models, seals, scoring, calibration, AI evidence, or exports.
- Scorer, corner, card, injury, observed-xG, and multi-match player-form model inputs remain
  unavailable without an approved point-in-time corpus and forward evaluation.
- Golavo remains a football-analysis workbench, not a betting product.

## Install and verify

The release workflow must publish the Apple Silicon DMG and updater archive, Windows x64 EXE
and MSI installers, `latest.json`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and detached signatures as one stable
release. The installers remain OS-unsigned and macOS is not notarized, so Gatekeeper or
SmartScreen may warn on first install. Updater payloads and the checksum ledger are signed.

Download only from the [official Golavo releases](https://github.com/udhawan97/Golavo/releases)
and compare manual downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
