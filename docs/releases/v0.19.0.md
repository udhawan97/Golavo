# Golavo v0.19.0 — A sharper match room

Golavo 0.19.0 turns the current source work into one bounded desktop milestone. The Match
Cockpit now opens with a compact deterministic Study Desk, top-five clubs gain an optional
provider-backed Transfer Desk, and the local integrity and club-continuity work completed after
0.18.0 joins the packaged product.

Golavo remains a match-analysis workbench, not a bet-placement or recommendation product. The
statistical engine owns every Golavo probability. AI may explain an accepted evidence bundle but
cannot verify, average, alter, or replace a forecast.

## What is new

- **Match Study Desk.** Compare the separate Elo and Dixon–Coles W/D/L voices, the council's
  largest percentage-point spread, the goal voice's most-likely exact score, over/under 2.5,
  both-teams-to-score, and both clean-sheet probabilities. `1 / probability` is labelled a
  model-implied mathematical equivalent with no margin, market movement, ranking, or
  recommendation.
- **Transfer Desk.** Separately enable the Sportmonks transfer capability, choose one club from
  Golavo's exact top-five current-season fixture identities, and fetch a bounded one-year
  arrival/departure window. Golavo reads at most four pages of 50 rows, marks a truncated window
  partial, preserves provider IDs and the nullable free-text amount, and stores no response bytes.
- **Completed-match Player Lens.** Completed fixtures now say **Fetch final player stats** and
  **Refresh final player stats**, retain provider fetch time, and explain that one fixture is not
  a multi-match player-form series.
- **My Teams and follow continuity.** Exact local club favorites now share the complete follow
  controller, current/projected points, one named season voice, five-match run-ins, guarded stakes,
  like-for-like change notes, local export/preview import, and a bounded recent follow-update inbox.
- **Trust Center and recovery.** Proof inspection, checksummed forecast-ledger archive/restore,
  entry-by-entry archive comparison, local checkpoints, backward-compatible chain continuation,
  disposable recovery rehearsal, refresh application receipts, and guarded calibration slices are
  now part of the milestone. Restore and checkpoint mutations remain private desktop actions.

## Transfer and provider boundaries

- Provider transfer amounts are nullable free text. The UI labels them
  **provider-reported amount — currency unspecified**.
- Currency, installments, add-ons, sell-ons, agent/intermediary fees, training rewards, and
  conditional consideration remain explicitly unavailable. Golavo does not infer or total them.
- Rumours, photos/logos, scraped websites, undocumented endpoints, hidden refresh, and background
  provider polling are excluded.
- Sportmonks predictions, prices, player data, and transfers cannot enter model fitting, forecast
  generation, seals, settlement, scoring, calibration, AI evidence, picks, archives, or exports.
- The connector is fixture-tested with synthetic provider responses. A live account's access still
  depends on its token, subscription, competition entitlement, and add-ons; this release makes no
  unverified credentialed-coverage claim.

## Deliberately deferred

Scorer, corner, and card forecasts are not filled with provider opinions or AI prose. They require
separately licensed point-in-time histories, target-specific deterministic models, leakage controls,
time-split evaluation, calibration, and abstention. The same evidence gate defers multi-match player
form and a full transaction payment breakdown from primary club/issuer disclosures.

There is no ranked wager, expected-value comparison, bookmaker/affiliate link, staking or bankroll
workflow, bet placement, or claim that any outcome is a “best bet.”

## Release verification

The release gate covers the full Python and UI suites, transfer/path/identity/pagination/cancellation
hardening, no-store/private routes, typecheck and production builds, provenance and license
isolation, deterministic rebuilds, browser accessibility/responsiveness, docs checks and links,
Cargo locked checks, packaged sidecar/desktop acceptance, version agreement, Graphify refresh,
two-round independent council review, and exact public artifact/signature verification.

Installers remain OS-unsigned and not notarized, so Gatekeeper or SmartScreen may warn on first
install. Updater payloads, active official pack manifests, and the aggregate checksum ledger retain
Golavo's signed release identity.
